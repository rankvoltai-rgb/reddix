Reddix — AI Reddit Lead Generation

Reddix is an AI-powered Reddit lead intelligence platform. It scans Reddit on demand, scores buying intent on a 0–100 scale, and drafts natural, context-aware replies so SaaS founders, agencies, and growth teams can turn Reddit conversations into qualified leads. Manual scans only — no auto-posting, no background polling.

Core capabilities

  • AI Intent Scanner — manual, on-demand scans of monitored subreddits
  • Qualification Engine — 0–100 intent scoring across hot, warm, research, low
  • Lead CRM — persistent saved leads with status lifecycle and triage
  • AI Copilot — 3-variant reply drafts (Direct, Story, Expert)
  • Subreddit Discovery — AI suggests relevant communities to monitor
