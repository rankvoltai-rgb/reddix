# REDAccs: research notes

Checked: 2026-10-27

## 1. Exists / status

- **Official URL:** https://redaccs.com/. The lead-finding dashboard lives at https://leads.redaccs.com/.
- **Status: active.** The site loaded on 2026-10-27. The blog index shows a last-modified date of 2026-03-01 [9]. The Thread Finder page was published 2026-02-26 and last modified 2026-05-29 [7].
- **It's the right product.** This is the Reddit-focused REDAccs. The site's structured data names it "Buy Reddit Accounts" [9]. I found no namesake.
- **Correction to our old copy.** We called it a "pay-per-use Reddit thread finder". That is only part of the picture. REDAccs is mainly a **marketplace selling Reddit accounts and paid engagement** (upvotes, comments, posts) [1][2][3][4][5][6]. Since early 2026 it has also offered a **Reddit lead finder, "Thread Finder"**, which runs on a wallet / pay-per-use model [7][8].

## 2. What it is

REDAccs sells aged and blank Reddit accounts. It also sells done-for-you engagement: upvotes, comments placed by its staff from its own accounts, and posts. Its newer Thread Finder tool is for marketers who want to find Reddit threads where people ask for recommendations, score them for buying intent, and reply. Comment help can be bought through the same REDAccs workflow [7].

## 3. How it works

**Thread Finder (lead finding)** [7][8]
- **Data:** Reddit only. The vendor claims a "100,000+ subreddit index". You enter a URL, keyword, competitor or product description, and it maps topics to likely subreddits.
- **Search + monitoring + AI:** it watches recommendation, comparison and help-request threads and mentions. AI matching tries to separate a passing mention from someone actively looking for a solution. Each lead shows a "lead potential" score, a traffic estimate, subreddit fit and a status. The page doesn't explain how these are calculated.
- **Alerts:** email digest, Slack, webhook, Telegram, Web Push.
- **Replies:** it gives starter reply templates. The page says it "is not an auto-comment bot" and doesn't auto-comment or run account automation [7]. But the leads.redaccs.com login page lists "One-Click Comments" to draft *and post* from the dashboard, without saying how posting works [8]. **The two pages conflict, so this is uncertain.**
- **Pay-per-use:** a free 7-day trial, then a wallet you top up for paid actions such as buying comments, with "no monthly commitment" [7]. Per-action prices and a minimum top-up are not published on the pages I opened.

**Paid engagement and accounts**
- **Comments:** you supply the thread URL. The page says: "Finding or choosing a thread is not included in this service." A person on REDAccs' staff posts each comment from REDAccs-controlled accounts. The vendor describes them as aged one year or more, with at least 200 karma and separate residential IPs [3][4].
- **Upvotes:** sold as credits ("1 credit = 1 vote"). Ordering links go to upvote.net [2].
- **Posts:** placed by hand from "selected" aged accounts, or from your own account on request [5].
- **Accounts:** credentials (username, password, email access) are emailed after payment. Payment by crypto or card [1][6][10].

## 6. Where it's stronger than a competitor might be

- **No subscription:** a free trial and pay-per-use wallet suit occasional users who don't want a monthly seat [7].
- **Wide alert coverage:** Telegram and Web Push on top of email, Slack and webhook [7].
- **Monitoring through to fulfilment in one place:** a buyer who doesn't want to write or post replies can buy placement from the same vendor. Whether that is a strength depends on the buyer's risk tolerance (see section 8).
- **Clearer disclosure than some peers:** the comments page openly says paid engagement breaks Reddit's rules and that removals happen [3][4].

## 7. Limits stated or evident from its own materials

- Thread Finder: no published per-action prices, trial limits, match caps, rate limits or data-retention terms [7]. The vendor says timing isn't guaranteed ("Many matches are surfaced within minutes") and Reddit outcomes aren't guaranteed [7].
- Traffic figures are estimates [7].
- Comments: thread finding is not included in the comment service. Moderator removals are "reviewed case by case". The 87–93% retention figure is "internal tracking". There's no guarantee of replies, traffic or sales [3][4].
- Posts: the page says REDAccs "does not promise risk-free placement". Refund terms are not mentioned on the posts page [5].
- Accounts: blank accounts have no refunds or replacements once credentials are delivered, though a "Replacement Guarantee" heading contradicts this [6]. Karma accounts are replaced only for bans "due to issues on our end (prior to your use)" [10].
- Pricing is internally inconsistent in several places: the upvote package counts, the $0.04 vs $0.045 rate, and the $0.30 vs $1.30 account price [2][6].
- Terms of service and a privacy policy are not linked from the leads dashboard login page [8].

## 8. What a buyer comparing it to a Reddit lead-finding tool needs to know

- **It's an account and engagement seller first.** The core catalogue is Reddit accounts, upvotes, comments and posts [1]. The lead finder is a newer add-on, from February 2026 [7].
- **REDAccs itself says paid engagement breaks Reddit's rules.** Its comments page states: "Paid engagement is not permitted under Reddit's rules." [3][4]. Its upvotes page, by contrast, claims the service "consistently adheres to Reddit's guidelines" [2]. Reddit's own policy pages could not be fetched from this environment (see the blocked list below). **Before publishing any claim about Reddit's policy, open them manually** and quote them directly.
- **The pay-per-use model is opaque.** A free 7-day trial, then wallet top-ups, with no published per-action prices [7].
- **Whether the lead tool posts is unclear.** One page says it doesn't auto-comment [7]. The dashboard login advertises "One-Click Comments" that post [8].
- **Purchased comments come from REDAccs-controlled accounts, not yours**, and the buyer has to bring the thread URL [3][4].
