# /alternatives/replydaddy: brief

## Job

Someone searched "replydaddy alternative" or "replydaddy vs …". ReplyDaddy is the closest product to Reddix: draft-only, never connects a Reddit account. The visitor needs a fair account of the real differences (finding, scoring, writing, tracking, price), not a takedown. Then offer the trial.

## Concept: "Two draft-only tools, compared fairly"

Lead with what's shared, as a Venn diagram, then the differences, then where ReplyDaddy is ahead, then cost at the visitor's own usage.

## Sections

1. Hero: centred copy over a Venn (only ReplyDaddy / both / only Reddix); stacks on phones.
2. What's the same: a prose column; the risk sits with whoever posts; Reddit's spam guidance applies to the user.
3. Five places they part ways: chapters with ReplyDaddy and Reddix answers side by side.
4. Where ReplyDaddy is ahead: a six-cell grid (free plan, new posts, schedules, voice, MCP, agency tier).
5. Cost at your usage (interactive): sliders for subreddits, keywords, scans, generated posts and projects → the tier that fits from its published limits (or custom), the limit that ruled out the tier below, and a year's cost next to Reddix. Notes on what Reddix doesn't do.
6. A note on the stale $99/$299/$799 prices in its page metadata.
7. FAQ (5, numbered column), then Sources and the close in one split band.

## Truth

- ReplyDaddy: RESEARCH.md §1–3 (active, draft-only, no account connection, scan logic and windows, Analysis Prompt with 70% relevance weight, persona from samples, Post Generator with rule checks, MCP "Nothing is posted"), §4 (rendered tiers $49/$199/$799 and $490/$1,990/$7,990 with limits; free plan "5 subreddits & 10 keywords" vs terms "discovery only"; 7-day trial on sign-up; LTD with BYOK, price unpublished; metadata $99/$299/$799 valid to 2025-12-31), §6.
- Reddix: ../reddix.ts plus product code already documented (statuses new/engaged/replied/converted/archived; save threshold 35; tone setting). No claim about subreddit limits, seats or projects.
- Not used: AI model names (rendered "Claude Haiku 5.5" vs metadata "Sonnet 4"; irrelevant and inconsistent); exact free-plan scan limits (unknown); the roadmap (errored).
