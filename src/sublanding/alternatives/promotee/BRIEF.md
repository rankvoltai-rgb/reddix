# /alternatives/promotee: brief

## Job

Someone searched "promotee alternative" or "promotee vs …". They're weighing software that automates their own Reddit accounts. The page is clear about what Promotee does that Reddix doesn't (DMs, lead lists, many accounts, a cheap start), prices a month with its published credits, and shows where the activity lands: on the accounts they connect. One action: start with Reddix (`/auth`).

## Concept: "Your accounts, run by software, or not"

The difference from the other automation pages is *whose* accounts. Promotee acts as the customer's own connected accounts, so the risk and the reputation are theirs. Every section returns to that: which jobs act as your account, how many actions a month would go out from them, what Reddit says about each activity.
