# Promotee — research notes

Checked: 2026-10-27. All claims below come from the vendor's own pages unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Exists / status

- **Official URL:** https://www.promotee.io
- **Status:** Active. The site is live with a free trial and sign-up at app.promotee.io. The footer reads "© 2026" and "engine status: operational" [1]. We found no public changelog.
- **Domain note:** AlternativeTo still lists the website as promotee.co [6]. That domain did not resolve (NXDOMAIN) when we checked on 2026-10-27. Product Hunt lists https://www.promotee.io/ [7], and that is the live Reddit product.
- **Namesake to avoid:** promotee.app is an unrelated interview-practice flashcard app ("Promotee by Yoko") [8].
- **Confirmed Reddit product:** Yes. The site calls itself "The Reddit Engine" [1]. Product Hunt shows launches on January 3, 2022 and January 2, 2023 [7].

## 2. What it is

Promotee is a Reddit outbound-automation platform for businesses, founders and agencies. It runs the whole loop for you: spotting buying signals, warming up Reddit accounts, posting AI comments, sending automated DM sequences, and collecting replies in one inbox [1][3]. It positions itself as a replacement for six separate tools: a signal tool, a scraper, an outreach tool, a warmup tool, an engagement tool and an inbox [1].

## 3. How it works

- **What it finds:**
  - **Listnr** tracks keywords and hashtags across Reddit posts and comments [1][3].
  - **Mentionr** finds "the posts your buyers are reading" [3].
  - **Scrapr** pulls leads from subreddit members, post commenters, upvoters and Reddit search results, or from a CSV import [1][3].
  - The site says it flags people who comment on a competitor's thread or post about your problem space [2].
- **Drafts or posts:** It acts on your behalf.
  - Mentionr "leaves a relevant, human-sounding AI comment" [3].
  - Campaigns are "outreach that runs itself": multi-step DM sequences with branching logic and A/B tests [3].
  - Warmup makes accounts browse, upvote, comment, post and handle DMs "like a real person" [1][3].
  - Whether you can review comments or DMs before they go out is not mentioned on its site.
- **Whose accounts:** Your own, or your clients'. The FAQ says you connect the Reddit accounts you want to run. Promotee then handles "warmup, proxying, personas, and per-account limits" [2].
  - Plans allow 1, up to 5, or up to 10 connected Reddit accounts [2].
  - The FAQ says every account you run, yours or your clients', sits in one workspace [2].
- **Infrastructure:** It runs on "our own automation infrastructure", and campaigns run on "dedicated cloud browsers" [1][3].
- **Software or service:** Self-serve software. Higher tiers add onboarding and a dedicated account manager [2].
- **Platforms:** Reddit only [1].

## 6. Where it's stronger than a draft-only tool might be

- **Scale and time:** It automates finding leads, commenting, DMing and follow-ups across up to 10 accounts. A draft-only tool needs a human to post each reply.
- **Outbound DMs:** It runs DM sequences and collects replies in one inbox. A reply-drafting tool usually doesn't cover private outreach at all.
- **Lead lists:** It scrapes commenters, upvoters and subreddit members into lists. A thread-finding tool surfaces conversations, not lists of people.
- **Agency use:** It runs client accounts in one workspace [2].
- **Low entry price:** The trial needs no card, and the entry tier is $9/month [2].

## 7. Limits stated or evident from its own materials

- Limits are credit-based: 100, 1,000 or 5,000 credits a month. AI comments are capped at 10 a day on Engage [2].
- No Auto-DMs on the Engage tier [2].
- You must bring your own Reddit accounts. The FAQ says older accounts with some history warm up fastest [2].
- Review or approval step before comments or DMs post: not mentioned on its site.
- Disclosure of brand affiliation in comments or DMs: not mentioned on its site.
- The site says Promotee is "not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Reddit" [1].
- The blog index shows no post dates, so recency could not be checked [9].

## 8. What a buyer comparing it to a draft-only tool needs to know

- **Automation runs on your accounts:** Warmup, comments, DMs and campaigns run from the Reddit accounts you connect [2]. Any enforcement action would land on those accounts. With a draft-only tool, a person writes and posts each reply.
- **Reddit's rule on authentic participation:** Rule 2 asks users to "participate authentically in communities where you have a personal interest" and not to spam or engage in content manipulation [10]. Warmup activity that is automated "like a real person" [3] is something buyers should weigh against this wording.
- **Automated use and data collection:** Reddit's User Agreement bars automated use, including via bots, that could "interfere with, disable, disrupt, overburden" the service. It also says scraping without Reddit's prior written consent is prohibited [11]. Buyers should check how Scrapr collects members, commenters and upvoters against this.
- **Running many accounts:** The User Agreement says users "will not license, sell, or transfer" their account without Reddit's written approval [11]. Running client accounts from one workspace is the vendor's stated model [2]. How that fits each account owner's obligations is for the buyer to judge.
- **Reddit enforcement:** The Reddit Rules list suspension of accounts among enforcement options [10]. Reddit's help-center pages on spam and content manipulation returned HTTP 403 to us, so we can't quote them.
