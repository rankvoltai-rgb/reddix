# /alternatives/redreach: brief

## Job

Someone searched "redreach alternative" or "redreach vs …". They either use Redreach or are choosing between Reddit lead tools. Show what each product does next once a thread is found, so they can pick on substance, then offer the Reddix trial.

## Concept: "Same threads, a different next step"

Both tools start from your website and find threads where people want a product like yours. They split afterwards: Redreach goes towards Google-ranked threads, push alerts and Outbound DMs; Reddix goes towards a 0–100 intent score, on-demand scans and three drafts. The hero draws that as a fork.

## Sections

1. Hero: left-set H1 + answer + CTA; full-width fork diagram (shared trunk → two branches).
2. Side by side: a ruled table (stacked cards on phones), "Checked October 2026".
3. Four differences: numbered long-form rows (what a good thread means; replies vs DMs; alerts vs scanning; how you pay).
4. Before you automate DMs: Redreach's own description of Outbound beside Reddit's spam policy, with no verdict.
5. Plan finder (interactive): tick needs → cheapest Redreach plan that covers them (real table) + a year's cost next to Reddix's two prices; notes on what Reddix lacks.
6. Which one fits: honest two-column verdict.
7. FAQ (5, two-column grid), Sources, ink closing band.

## Truth

- Redreach: RESEARCH.md §1 (spelling, active, changelog 2026-09-08), §2–3 (website + competitors setup, Google-ranked threads, relevance score, alerts by plan, reply suggestions you post, no auto-posting, Outbound DM extension, delays/limits, 10–20/day advice), §4 (prices from the prod config; A/B hook → "as listed on its site in October 2026"; no trial; 48-hour refund; $12 pass), §7 (weekly on Startup).
- Reddix: ../reddix.ts (setup, scanning, alerts none, replies, posting, pricing). API/MCP isn't compared: Reddix has an MCP function not described in reddix.ts.
- Reddit: ../REDDIT-POLICY.md (Spam, flagged accounts). Placed next to Outbound neutrally; no claim that Redreach breaks rules, and no claim that Reddix is "safe".
- Not used: legacy $29 plan, experiment prices, multiple-account behaviour (not mentioned on its site).

## How it differs from the other two pages

- ReplyDaddy is the near-twin: a Venn hero, "what's the same" first, and a usage-based cost model.
- ReplyAgent is about who posts: a two-lane hero, a vendor-accounts section, and a posting budget calculator.
- Redreach is about the step after finding: a fork hero, a DM policy section, and a needs-based plan finder.
