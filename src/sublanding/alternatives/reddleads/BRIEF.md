# /alternatives/reddleads — brief

## Job
Someone searching "reddleads alternative" is comparing two tools that look alike on paper: both start from your website, score Reddit posts for intent, draft what to say, and never post. The real difference is rhythm. ReddLeads scans on a timer and alerts you; Reddix scans when you press Scan. The page makes that difference concrete and fair.

## Concept: "A scanner that never stops, or one you start"
The hero's fork diagram shows one shared first step (your website) splitting into two paths. The centrepiece is a 24-hour timeline that plays out when each tool would first see a buyer's post.
