## 2. What it is

ReddLeads is an AI Reddit lead finder aimed at freelancers, SaaS founders and small businesses [1][2]. You enter your website. Its AI works out your niche and ideal customer and builds a campaign of subreddits to watch [1]. It then scans those subreddits around the clock and scores each post for buying or hiring intent. Matching leads go into a feed with an opener suggestion; you do the outreach yourself [1][3].

## 3. How it works

- **Data sources / platforms:** Reddit only. The tool scans the subreddits you choose [3]. Coverage is per subreddit, not all of Reddit: 7/15/35 subreddits by plan [2]. Whether it uses Reddit's official API is not mentioned on its site.
- **Keywords vs search vs AI scoring:** mainly AI intent scoring. The homepage says "Keywords were a thing of the past." [1] Every thread gets an intent score, and you can adjust score thresholds [3]. The features page also lists "Keyword tuning and priority settings" [3]. Auto-scan runs every 30/15/5 minutes depending on plan [2].
- **Alert channels:** in-app feed on every plan [2]. Instant alerts to Discord, Telegram and Slack, plus a daily or weekly digest email, start at Pro [2]. The features page names only Discord and Telegram [3].
- **Reply drafting:** yes. It writes "context-aware, non-spammy opener suggestions" for each post [1], and "warm outreach" messages based on the post and your offer [1].
- **Posting / automation on Reddit:** none. The homepage says "We never auto-reply or auto-DM." [1]
- **Multiple accounts:** not mentioned on its site.
- **Other:** lead CRM with contacted/skipped/active statuses [3], analytics [2][3], CSV export on Max [2].

## 6. Where it's stronger than a competitor might be

- **Simple, transparent pricing:** three flat monthly tiers with published limits, and a 7-day trial on each [2].
- **Fast scanning on paid tiers:** every 5 minutes on Max, 15 on Pro [2]. This suits people who want to reply early.
- **Discord alerts:** less common among Reddit lead tools, and available from Pro [2].
- **Hands-off setup:** you start from a website URL rather than building a keyword list [1].
- **Clear no-automation stance:** "We never auto-reply or auto-DM." [1]

## 7. Limits stated or evident from its own materials

- Coverage is capped by subreddit count (7/15/35). It is not a Reddit-wide search [2].
- Starter has no instant alerts or digest email; it is in-app feed only [2].
- Daily evaluation caps (200/2,000/10,000 leads) [2].
- Annual plans: not mentioned on its site.
- Platforms other than Reddit: not mentioned on its site.
- Brand or competitor mention tracking: not mentioned on its site.
- Team seats or multiple users: not mentioned on its site. The features page mentions "custom delivery settings per user" [3].
- Company identity, terms and refund text were not readable without JavaScript [4][5].
- Vendor claims without published backing: "Reddit Ban Chance: 0%", "10K+ Leads Found Daily", "85% Avg Intent Score" [1].

## 8. What a buyer comparing it to another Reddit lead-finding tool needs to know

- It is a pure lead finder: subreddit monitoring, intent scoring, alerts, opener drafts. It does no posting or DMing [1].
- You watch a fixed number of subreddits (7–35). A tool that searches all of Reddit, or matches keywords across it, may catch posts in subreddits you didn't pick [2].
- Instant alerts start at $29.99/mo (Pro). The $19.99 Starter is in-app only [2].
- The product is young: the AlternativeTo listing is from March 2026 and the homepage shows "50+ beta testers" [1][6]. The company behind it isn't named on readable pages.
- Every plan has a 7-day trial, so a side-by-side test is cheap [2].
