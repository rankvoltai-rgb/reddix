# CommunityTracker — research notes

Checked: 2026-10-27. All claims below come from the vendor's own pages unless marked otherwise.

## 1. Exists / status

- **Official URL:** https://communitytracker.ai
- **Status:** Active. The site is live with a free plan, paid plans and a trial [1][2]. Its blog has posts dated Oct 27, 2026 [4]. No changelog link appears on the homepage [1].
- **Confirmed Reddit product:** Yes, but Reddit is one of 10+ monitored platforms, not the focus. Its homepage describes it as account intelligence plus social monitoring for B2B sales and marketing teams [1].

## 2. What it is

CommunityTracker is a B2B signal and outbound platform. It enriches and ranks target accounts by buying signals, and runs LinkedIn and email outreach. It also monitors public communities, including Reddit, for posts where people are asking for a product like yours [1]. It is pitched at founders doing their own sales, SDR teams, agencies, and marketing teams that want share-of-voice reports [1]. Reddit lead-finding is one feature inside a wider sales tool.

## 3. How it works

- **Data sources and platforms:** Reddit, LinkedIn, X, Hacker News, GitHub, Stack Overflow, Dev.to, Indie Hackers, Slack and Discord [1][3]. Which platforms you get depends on the plan. Free is Reddit only, Starter covers four platforms, and Pro and above cover all 10+ [2]. Account intelligence enriches leads on 30+ data points, such as role, company size, funding, open roles and verified email [1].
- **Keywords vs search vs AI scoring:** You pick keywords, competitors and problems to watch. Each post is scored for buying intent against your ICP and the problem you solve, and labelled Hot or Warm with a number and a reason [3]. The pricing page lists "AI intent scoring and filtering" under Pro, not under Free or Starter [2]. Scan frequency is not stated on the monitoring page [3]. The Free and Starter plans list "daily alerts" [2].
- **Author identification:** The vendor says it finds the post author and supplies a work email and LinkedIn profile when available [1].
- **Alert channels:** Slack alerts to a channel you choose, including the post, score and reason [1][3]. The Pro plan lists "daily Slack alerts" [2]. HubSpot sync turns Hot and Warm posts into deals [1]. Leads from chosen keywords can be added to a campaign automatically [3].
- **Analytics/reports:** Share of voice by community and week, competitor mentions and visibility gaps (Pro and up). A share-of-voice PDF export and "visibility gaps and action plan" are on Advanced [1][2]. "Community intelligence searches" are capped at 3 or 5 a month [2].
- **AI assistant / integrations:** Connects to Claude, ChatGPT and Grok, so you can ask questions about your signals [1]. It integrates with data providers (Apollo, Prospeo, Icypeas, Leadsforge, using your own keys) and senders (Instantly, Smartlead, Salesforge, lemlist) [1]. The homepage links to an API documentation page [1], which I did not open.
- **Drafts replies:** Yes. For community posts, the reply is drafted for you to edit and post yourself [1][3]. Starter lists "reply drafts for posts," and Pro lists "AI reply drafts" [2].
- **Posts anything:** Not to communities. The monitoring page says CommunityTracker "never posts on your behalf in a community" [3]. It can send LinkedIn and email outreach, either after you approve each message or automatically as a campaign, with an optional teammate-approval step [1].

## 6. Where it's stronger than a focused lead tool might be

- **Breadth of sources:** Reddit plus nine other communities, including Hacker News, GitHub, Stack Overflow and Discord. This suits buyers whose prospects aren't only on Reddit [3].
- **Contact enrichment:** It tries to find the author's work email and LinkedIn profile, which a Reddit-only tool usually can't do [1].
- **Full outbound workflow:** Enrichment, account ranking, LinkedIn and email sequences, a unified inbox and HubSpot sync all live in one tool [1].
- **Marketing reporting:** Share of voice against competitors, with PDF export on Advanced [1][2].
- **Free tier:** A permanent $0 plan for a single Reddit keyword [2].
- **Integrations ecosystem:** Many data-provider, sending and AI-assistant integrations [1].

## 7. Limits stated or evident from its own materials

- Keyword caps are tight: 1 keyword on Free, 4 on Starter, 10 on Pro and 20 on Advanced [2].
- AI intent scoring and filtering is listed only from Pro ($99/mo) upward [2]. The Free and Starter listings don't include it.
- Free and Starter alerts are listed as "daily," and Pro lists "daily Slack alerts" [2]. Real-time alerting is not mentioned on its site. Scan frequency is not stated [3].
- Starter covers only Reddit, LinkedIn, Hacker News and GitHub [2].
- Community intelligence searches are capped at 3 or 5 a month [2].
- Credit allotments per plan are not stated [2].
- No Reddit-specific features are described. Reddit is handled like the other platforms [3].
- The trial is short: 3 days [2].
- A changelog is not mentioned on its site [1].

## 8. What a buyer comparing it to a Reddit lead-finding tool needs to know

- It is a multi-platform B2B sales tool, and Reddit is one input. If Reddit is your main channel, the Reddit-specific depth may matter more than the breadth.
- The cheapest plan with AI intent scoring is $99/mo, which allows 10 keywords. Free and Starter appear to be keyword monitoring without the AI filter, going by the plan listings.
- Alerts on the listed plans are daily. If you need to reply to Reddit threads within minutes, check how fresh its alerts really are during the 3-day trial.
- It drafts community replies but never posts them to communities. It can send LinkedIn and email outreach automatically if you turn that on.
- Its strengths are contact enrichment, outbound sequencing, HubSpot sync and share-of-voice reporting. These matter more to sales teams than to founders who mainly want to join Reddit threads.
