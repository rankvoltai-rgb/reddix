# /alternatives/communitytracker — brief

## Job
Someone searching "communitytracker alternative" is usually deciding between breadth (many communities, plus enrichment and outbound) and a tool that only does Reddit. The page shows that trade plainly and prices it for their own setup.

## Concept: "Ten platforms, or one done closely"
The hero's coverage strip puts ten community chips, each tagged with the first plan that includes it, above a single Reddit chip with what Reddix does there. The page then lays the two workflows side by side (they share one step out of five), climbs CommunityTracker's plan ladder, and ends with a calculator that prices the visitor's own setup.

## Sections
1. Hero: H1 beside a plain answer and CTAs; the coverage strip; a "Checked October 2026" line with a sources link.
2. Workflow: two vertical five-step rails, with the shared step marked on both.
3. Plans: a four-step staircase (Free → Advanced), marked where AI intent scoring starts (Pro), plus notes on daily alerts and credits.
4. Calculator (interactive): keywords slider (with plan caps at their true positions), community chips and toggles (AI scoring, Slack, PDF) give the cheapest CommunityTracker plan, which inputs set it, and 12-month cost. Reddix shows Premium × 12 and Lifetime, and lists what it can't cover.
5. Verdict: two scorecard rows, each a title cell plus five reasons.
6. Questions (5): a ruled question/answer table.
7. Close on soft red, with sources beside it.

## Truth
- CommunityTracker (RESEARCH.md §3–§8): 10+ platforms; Free = Reddit; Starter = Reddit, LinkedIn, HN, GitHub; Pro+ = all. Keywords 1/4/10/20. $0/$39/$99/$199 a month, monthly, annual on request. AI intent scoring and filtering from Pro. Alerts "daily" (Slack daily on Pro); scan frequency not stated. Author email and LinkedIn lookup. LinkedIn/email outreach (approved or automatic). HubSpot sync. Share of voice (Pro), PDF (Advanced). 3-day Pro trial, 14-day money-back. Credits: 100 to start, per-plan amounts not stated. "Never posts on your behalf in a community."
- Reddix (../reddix.ts): Reddit only; $45/mo or $150 once; 7-day Premium trial; no free plan; on-demand scans; no alerts; never posts or connects an account; three drafts; profile from URL. "Doesn't sell by keyword" (scan-reddit writes its own search phrases) and "doesn't look up who wrote a post" are from the code.
- Not used: which plan includes author lookup (not stated, so it's left out of the calculator); the API docs page (not opened); the "AI voice agent" (coming soon).

## How it differs from the other two pages
- Subreddit Signals: near-identical twins, with an overlap map and a needs checklist. ReddLeads: scan cadence, with a 24-hour timeline. This page is the breadth-vs-depth argument, with a workflow comparison and a cost calculator.
