# Reddit lead-gen / monitoring tools missing from our competitor list

Checked: 2026-10-27

Our current list: GummySearch, RedReach, Redditor AI, SubredditSignals, CommunityTracker, ReplyAgent, ReddLeads, F5Bot, Syften, CatchIntent, Brandwatch, ReplyDaddy, Promotee, RedditFlow, LeadHunter, Linkeddit, REDAccs, RedditMonitor, Reddar.

Each tool below is missing from that list. I opened each site on 2026-10-27. "Active in 2026" is based on what the page itself shows.

1. **Octolens**: https://octolens.com/. AI social listening that collects brand mentions across the web, including Reddit, and delivers them through a REST API, webhooks and an MCP server. It's aimed at founders, marketing and DevRel teams. No copyright year, but the page references "verified 2026 pricing" and a 2026 comparison, so likely active.
2. **KWatch.io**: https://kwatch.io/. Real-time keyword alerts for Reddit, LinkedIn, X, Facebook, YouTube and Hacker News, pitched for sales leads, brand mentions and competitor tracking. Footer "© 2026 KWatch.io".
3. **SnitchFeed**: https://snitchfeed.com/. AI social listening that scans Reddit, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky and Hacker News for buying intent and competitor dissatisfaction, then scores and routes leads to GTM teams. Footer "© 2026 Incentyvise LLC".
4. **Brand24**: https://brand24.com/. AI social listening and brand monitoring across social media, news, blogs, forums and reviews, with Reddit among its sources. Aimed at marketers, PR teams and agencies. Footer "Copyrights © 2026 Brand24 Global, Inc."
5. **Awario**: https://awario.com/. Brand monitoring across social, news, blogs, forums and reviews. It has a dedicated Reddit monitoring page and lists sales teams among its audiences. Footer "© 2026".
6. **Leado**: https://leado.co/. A Reddit AI agent that monitors subreddits for buying signals and drafts outreach replies. Its PeerPush listing was published Dec 5, 2025 and was #2 Product of the Day on Dec 6, 2025 (https://peerpush.com/p/reddit-ai-agent-for-lead-generation). **Uncertain: the homepage shows no 2026 date, so I couldn't confirm it's active in 2026.**

Other tools surfaced in research but not opened or not verified, so not listed above: ReplyGuy (https://replyguy.com/, opened, covers Reddit, but no date confirms it's active in 2026), Redreach-alternatives entries on AlternativeTo such as Leedlime, Bazzly, FlipSignal, Threadlytics, Alertly and RedReplier (https://alternativeto.net/software/redreach/?p=2, opened, last updated Aug 8, 2026; I did not visit those products' own sites), and LeadRadar, RedditScout and Reddiscout (search results only).
