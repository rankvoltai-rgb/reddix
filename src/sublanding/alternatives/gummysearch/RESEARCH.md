# GummySearch: research notes

Checked 2026-10-27. All facts below come from pages opened on that date (see Sources). Page content was read through a fetch tool that summarises pages, so short quotes should be spot-checked against the live page before they are published.

## 1. Exists / status

- Official URL: https://gummysearch.com/ [1]
- Status: **closed to new users. A full shutdown is scheduled.** The homepage banner reads "GummySearch is closed as of 11/30/2025." [1]
- Official timeline from the closure help article [2]:
  - Until 2025-11-30: full access, sign-ups and purchases open.
  - 2025-12-01 to 2026-11-30: existing paid customers keep access until their billing period ends. No new sign-ups, purchases or renewals. Free users can only view, download or delete their data.
  - **2026-12-01: complete shutdown of all services and deletion of all user data.**
- Stated reason: the company could not reach a commercial licence agreement for Reddit's Data API [2]. The founder's closing post (signed "Fed", dated 2025-11-30) says GummySearch is shutting down to comply with Reddit's API policies [3].
- As of today (2026-10-27), only remaining paid or lifetime-deal users can still log in. The closing post says lifetime-deal access runs to 2026-11-30 [3]. Everything goes dark in about five weeks.

## 2. What it is

GummySearch was a Reddit audience-research tool for founders, marketers and investors. It helped them find the subreddits where their customers gather and surface the conversations that matter. Its homepage lists four use cases: generating startup ideas, validating demand, finding content ideas and finding sales leads [1]. The founder says more than 140,000 founders, marketers and investors relied on it [3]. It started as a solo indie project [3].

## 3. How it works

- **Data sources:** Reddit only, per the pages opened. The product page cites coverage of 130,000 active communities and "millions of daily conversations happening on Reddit" [4]. The closure notices tie its data access to Reddit's Data API [2][3].
- **Search vs. alerts vs. AI:**
  - Users build "audiences" (groups of subreddits) and search conversations within one audience or across all of Reddit, with filters and visualised results [4].
  - "Themes" group conversations into categories such as Pain Points, Solution Requests, Money Talk, Hot Discussions and Seeking Alternatives [4].
  - Tracked keywords notify users about relevant conversations, such as category requests, competitor complaints and brand mentions [4].
  - Whether AI was used for theme classification or scoring is not stated on the pages opened.
- **Alert channels:** not stated on the pages opened (email, Slack or webhook are not mentioned) [4].
- **Posting or automation on Reddit:** the product page does not say GummySearch posts. It recommends a Reddit account to take part in conversations users find, which implies the user posts manually [4].
- **Multiple accounts:** not mentioned on its site.

## 4. Pricing (checked 2026-10-27)

**Not purchasable.** New purchases and renewals stopped after 2025-11-30 [2]. The pricing page still lists these tiers, as historical reference [5]:

| Tier | Price shown | Main limit shown |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0/month | 50 keyword searches |
| Starter | $29/month | Unlimited keyword searches |
| Pro | $59/month | No numeric limits listed |
| Mega | $199/month | "Enterprise" usage limits, not specified |

- The page has a monthly/annual toggle advertising 33% savings on annual billing. Annual prices were not captured [5].
- Day passes also existed. The closure article mentions buying them before the cutoff [2], but no price was captured.

## 6. Where it was stronger than a focused Reddit lead tool

- **Research depth:** GummySearch was built for market research (idea validation, pain-point discovery and audience sizing), not just lead alerts. The theme buckets and audience organisation suited founders exploring a market [4].
- **Scale and reputation:** it reports over 140,000 users [3] and was widely known among indie founders.
- **Free tier:** it had a $0 plan with 50 keyword searches [5].

These strengths are now moot for new buyers, because the product cannot be bought [2].

## 7. Limits stated or evident from its own materials

- Closed to new sign-ups since 2025-11-30. All services shut down and all data is deleted on 2026-12-01 [2].
- The vendor states it could not secure a commercial Reddit Data API licence [2].
- Reddit only, per the pages opened. No other platforms are mentioned [1][4].
- Alert channels and AI scoring are not mentioned on the pages opened.

## 8. What a buyer comparing it to a Reddit lead-finding tool needs to know

- You cannot buy GummySearch. Sign-ups closed on 2025-11-30, and the service and all user data are deleted on 2026-12-01 [2].
- The vendor's own stated reason for closing is the lack of a commercial Reddit Data API licence [2]. That makes data access a fair question to ask any Reddit tool you evaluate.
- Existing users should export their data before 2026-12-01 [2].
- GummySearch's own alternatives page lists replacement tools in categories including "Reddit Lead Generation." It discloses that some links may be affiliate links [6].
- The founder says they plan to build more market-research products in 2026, but no successor product is named [3].
