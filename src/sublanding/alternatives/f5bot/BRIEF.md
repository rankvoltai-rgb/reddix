# /alternatives/f5bot: brief

## Job
Someone searching **f5bot alternative** already uses keyword alerts, or is weighing them. They want to know whether to keep alerting on keywords or move to something that ranks posts. The page answers honestly: F5Bot is the better tool for every mention, alerts and Hacker News; Reddix is for a short list of Reddit posts from likely buyers.

## Concept: "Every mention, or the ones ready to buy"
The difference is shown, not asserted. The **inbox demo** takes six fictional posts that all match one tracked phrase ("invoice reminders"), shows them in arrival order as an alert would, then reorders them with Reddix's real first-pass rules (`features/intent-detection/signals.ts`: competitor and buying-phrase priority, junk skipping, signal points). It's labelled as the rule pass only, with no AI score, and as fictional.

## Truth
- F5Bot: platforms, scanning and matching model, no regex/wildcards (RESEARCH §3, §7; f5bot.com/faq). Tiers, prices, caps, delays and commercial-use labels (RESEARCH §4; f5bot.com/tiers). AI filters on Gold and above, 2 or 5 filters, $10 token allowance (RESEARCH §3, §4; docs-semantic). Running since 2017 (RESEARCH §1).
- Reddix: everything through `../reddix` (pricing, Reddit-only, on-demand scanning, no alerts, scoring, three drafts, never posts).
- Not used: the 430,000 alerts/day figure (true per About, but irrelevant); third-party $9.99 Silver price (contradicts vendor).

## How it differs from syften and brandwatch
- F5Bot is about the alert-vs-score loop and the commercial-use line on pricing; its demo is the inbox reorder.
- Syften is about coverage breadth (sources) and has a planner.
- Brandwatch is about buyer type and data latency, and has a reply clock.
Each has its own hero composition, comparison form, FAQ layout and closing band.
