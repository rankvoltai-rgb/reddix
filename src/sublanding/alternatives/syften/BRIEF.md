# /alternatives/syften: brief

## Job
Someone searching **syften alternative** is choosing between broad, multi-platform keyword monitoring and a Reddit-specific lead tool. The page makes the trade-off explicit: Syften covers about 17 sources plus the web; Reddix covers only Reddit but scores and drafts. Its purpose is to help the reader pick, including picking Syften.

## Concept: "Coverage vs depth"
The hero is a wall of every source Syften lists, with LinkedIn struck through and X/YouTube marked as add-ons. Reddix's single source sits beneath it. The **planner** lets readers tick where their buyers talk and set filter count, AI and Slack needs. It reports which tool covers what, an honest verdict and the smallest Syften plan that fits, add-ons included (`plan.ts`, from Syften's published limits).

## Sections
1. Hero: centred copy over the source wall.
