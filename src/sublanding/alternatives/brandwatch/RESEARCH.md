# Brandwatch: research notes

Checked 2026-10-27. All facts below come from pages opened on that date (see Sources). Page content was read through a fetch tool that summarises pages, so short quotes should be spot-checked against the live page before they are published.

## 1. Exists / status

- Official URL: https://www.brandwatch.com/ [1]
- Status: **active.** It is part of Cision, which closed its acquisition of Brandwatch on 2021-06-01 and combined it with Falcon.io [9]. The current site carries a "Brandwatch + Cision" enterprise offering [1].
- **Recent Reddit change:** a Brandwatch help-centre article describes changes to Reddit coverage effective **2026-08-28**. Legacy Reddit historical data stopped being available after **2026-09-30** [5]. See sections 3 and 7.

## 2. What it is

Brandwatch is an enterprise consumer-intelligence and social-media-management suite for brands, agencies, and PR and research teams [1][2]. Its core jobs are large-scale social listening and analysis across many networks and the wider web, plus publishing, engagement, influencer marketing and reporting [2]. It is built for analysing brand and market conversation at scale, not specifically for finding individual sales leads.

## 3. How it works

- **Products:**
  - Consumer Intelligence (Consumer Research), Search & GenAI and Media Intelligence [2].
  - Social Media Management, formerly Falcon: Listen, Publish, Engage, Advertise, Measure and Benchmark [2].
  - Influencer Marketing, formerly Paladin [2].
- **Data coverage (multi-platform):**
  - The site cites "over 100m sources" [1].
  - The Consumer Intelligence page cites 1.7 trillion historical conversations back to 2010 and 501 million new conversations a day [3]. This was read from a page that may have resolved to the homepage, so treat it as uncertain.
  - The data-networks page lists TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky, Pinterest, Tumblr, YouTube, Twitch, APAC networks, broadcast, news, blogs, forums, reviews and more [4].
- **Reddit:**
  - The "Our Suite" page lists Reddit as a data network [2], but the main data-networks page opened did not mention Reddit [4]. The old Reddit network URL returned 404 [10].
  - The help article confirms Reddit is still ingested in Consumer Research (BCR) and SMM Listen. It expects about 400 million Reddit posts a month and 10 million posts and comments a day. It also says complete subreddit coverage and every post and comment cannot be guaranteed [5].
  - **Latency after 2026-08-28:** about 55% of Reddit content arrives within 1 hour, 81% within 2 hours and 89% within 3 hours. Some content takes up to about 30 hours [5].
  - **History:** BCR retains 13 months of Reddit history, backfilled progressively. SMM Listen retains 12 months, or 24 months with a premium add-on [5].
  - The article gives no reason for the coverage change [5].
  - History: Brandwatch added Reddit data in 2017 [7]. It joined Reddit's Official Partner Program in January 2023, and its 2023 release cited full firehose access and Reddit compliance certification [6][8]. Whether those 2023 terms still apply after the August 2026 change is **uncertain**. The help article does not say.
- **Search, alerts and AI:**
  - Boolean query-based listening, including a subreddit operator [5].
  - Iris AI offers conversational search, summaries, anomaly explanations and a query writer that suggests subreddits [11].
  - Consumer Intelligence offers AI-powered email alerts to stakeholders [3]. The help article says alerts still include Reddit content, subject to the new latency [5].
- **Alert channels:** email alerts are confirmed [3]. Slack or webhook alerts were not mentioned on the pages opened. APIs exist [2][5].
- **Posting or automation:** the Social Media Management suite publishes and engages on social networks through a content calendar and a unified inbox [1][2]. Iris can draft posts and suggest replies [11]. **Whether it can publish or reply on Reddit specifically is not confirmed** on any page opened.
- **Multiple accounts:** not mentioned on its site in a Reddit context.

## 6. Where it's stronger than a focused Reddit lead tool

- **Breadth:** dozens of networks plus news, broadcast, blogs, forums and reviews in one place [4]. A Reddit-only tool can't match this.
- **Enterprise analytics:** historical trend analysis, sentiment and emotion, audience panels, dashboards and live visualisations, and custom classifiers [3][5].
- **Full suite:** listening, publishing, engagement inbox, ads, influencer marketing and reporting under one vendor [2].
- **Reddit audience research:** Reddit Social Panels and subreddit-level analysis suit brand and market research teams [5][6].
- **Formal Reddit relationship (historical):** it was an Official Reddit Partner from 2023 [6][8]. Current status is uncertain (see section 3).

## 7. Limits stated or evident from its own materials

- **Pricing is not public.** It is sold through demos, with "standard to enterprise plans" [12].
- **Reddit changes effective 2026-08-28** [5]:
  - Latency is now hours, not minutes: about 55% within 1 hour, with a tail up to about 30 hours.
  - Retention is 13 months in BCR and 12 months in SMM Listen (24 with an add-on). Legacy Reddit history is gone after 2026-09-30.
  - Complete coverage is not guaranteed.
  - Removed: Reddit Score, upvote percentage, post and author flair, NSFW, subreddit subscribers and topics, Engagement Score for Reddit, and Potential Audience. A new Potential Audience is planned for Q1 2027.
  - Comment count now counts only root-post comments.
  - The SMM Listen Boolean operator set for Reddit is limited to the subreddit operator.
- Reddit publishing or replying from the suite is not mentioned on its site.
- Slack or webhook alert delivery was not mentioned on the pages opened.
- Lead scoring for individual buyers is not mentioned on its site. Its lead-generation blog post frames social listening as research input [13].

## 8. What a buyer comparing it to a Reddit lead-finding tool needs to know

- Brandwatch is an enterprise listening and analytics suite. Its pricing isn't public and you buy it through a sales demo [12]. Expect a different budget and buying process from a self-serve Reddit tool.
- Since 2026-08-28, its own help centre says Reddit data can arrive hours late, with up to about 30 hours for a small share [5]. For catching a Reddit thread while it's fresh enough to reply, that latency matters.
- Reddit history in Brandwatch is now capped at 12–13 months (24 with an SMM add-on), and several Reddit metrics, including score, were removed [5].
- It is strongest when you need cross-network brand, market and audience analysis, or a full social suite. It is not a Reddit lead inbox.
- If you already pay for Brandwatch, its Reddit alerts and subreddit operator may cover basic monitoring [5]. Check latency and coverage against your use case.
