# /alternatives/brandwatch: brief

## Job
Someone searching **brandwatch alternative for reddit** is usually either a smaller team priced out of an enterprise suite, or a Brandwatch user affected by the August 2026 Reddit coverage change. The page explains that the two are built for different buyers, sets out the Reddit change from Brandwatch's own help centre, and says plainly when Brandwatch is the right choice.

## Concept: "Different buyer, different clock"
The hero carries a ruled fact sheet (owner, pricing, coverage, Reddit latency and history). The **reply clock** lets readers pick a reply window and states only what Brandwatch's published arrival points (55% at 1h, 81% at 2h, 89% at 3h, tail to about 30h) support. Below one hour it says no figure is published; between points it says "at least". Reddix is described as finding posts when you scan, with no claim of speed or completeness.
