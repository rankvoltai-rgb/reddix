# /alternatives/catchintent: brief

## Job

Someone searched **catchintent alternative**. Either they found CatchIntent in an older "best Reddit tools" list, or they're a legacy customer. They need to learn that CatchIntent no longer sells Reddit monitoring, what it is now, what legacy customers keep, and what to use for Reddit, including what Reddix won't bring back.

## Concept

**The product that changed.** A left-set headline over a before/after diptych, hinged on v3.0.0 (May 13, 2026). The interactive piece is a date slider over CatchIntent's dated changelog, with releases as jump buttons: for any day, it shows each platform's status for a new buyer (not yet / proof of concept / watched / existing customers only). The page then covers the legacy-customer position, a side-by-side of CatchIntent today and Reddix, an honest 2×2 of old features Reddix lacks, and a note on using both.
